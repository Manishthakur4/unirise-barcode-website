FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide warranty on all products?

Yes, all products we supply carry the full manufacturer's warranty — typically 1 year for most hardware. Zebra products come with Zebra's standard warranty, Honeywell products with Honeywell's warranty, and so on. We also offer extended warranty and AMC contracts for additional peace of mind.

Do you ship products across all of India?

Yes, we deliver to all major cities and towns across India via courier and freight services. Delivery typically takes 1-3 business days for metro cities and 3-5 business days for other locations. Bulk and custom orders may require additional lead time.

Can I get a product demo before buying?

Absolutely. We provide free product demonstrations at our Delhi office, or our sales engineers can visit your premises for on-site demos in major cities. For customers in other cities, we offer video demos and can ship demo units on request for qualified projects.

Do you offer installation and training?

Yes, we provide professional installation and user training for all equipment. Basic installation and training is included for orders above a certain value. Our certified engineers ensure your team is fully trained before we close a project.

What is your response time for technical support?

For AMC customers, we guarantee a 4-hour telephone/remote response and next-business-day on-site response in metro cities. For non-AMC customers, we provide best-effort support. We recommend taking an AMC contract for business-critical equipment to ensure minimal downtime.

Do you provide GST invoices?

Yes, all purchases come with a proper GST-compliant tax invoice. We are a registered GST entity and can provide invoices suitable for Input Tax Credit (ITC) claims by your business.

Can you handle government tenders?

Yes, we regularly participate in and supply to government tenders at central and state levels, including PSUs, hospitals, educational institutions and defense establishments. We are registered on GeM portal and can provide all necessary documentation for government procurement.
