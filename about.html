India's Most Trusted Barcode Technology Partner

Unirise Barcode Solutions Pvt. Ltd. was founded with a single mission: to make world-class barcode and auto-ID technology accessible to Indian businesses of all sizes. Based in Noida, U.P., we have grown into one of India's most trusted names in the barcode and RFID industry.

Today, we serve over 2,500 businesses across 28+ cities — from small retail shops to Fortune 500 companies. Our comprehensive product portfolio covers everything from simple handheld scanners to complex RFID warehouse systems, backed by certified technical support.

As an authorized dealer for Zebra, Honeywell, Datalogic, TSC, Citizen and other global brands, we ensure every product we supply is 100% genuine, carries full manufacturer warranty, and is supported by our in-house technical team.

  • Authorized partner for all major barcode & RFID brands in India
  • Certified engineers for installation, training and AMC support
  • Pan-India sales and service network across 28+ cities
  • Large in-stock inventory for fast delivery and fulfillment
  • GST-compliant invoicing for smooth B2B procurement
  • Dedicated account management for every enterprise client
